## 1. Correct and freeze comparison sensitivity before model scores

The A04 scorer is correct for its stated matching rule, but the current selection criterion is insensitive for the delivered mixture pools. Each development mixture query searches one eight-second asset for a 2.4-second target. For a regular 2.4-second window grid with 0.25-second hop plus a final end-aligned window, there are 24 candidate intervals. Under the frozen IoU >=0.5 rule:

| Development source-consistency condition | Queries | Windows matching at least one target | Constant-score chronological hit@10 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| -12 dB | 8 | 8 | 100% |
| 0 dB | 8 | 7 | 100% |
| +12 dB | 8 | 7 | 100% |

Uniform random ranking has expected hit@10 of **99.2028%**, macro-averaged across these conditions. This follows from `1 - C(N-m, K) / C(N, K)`, where N is the interval count and m the number matching any target. Constant chronological ranking scores **66.6667% at K=1**; random ranking averages **30.5556% at K=1**. These are model-independent calculations from development geometry, not measured acoustic quality, and demonstrate why top-10 coverage cannot establish progress here.

This prompt explicitly requires a narrow **pre-comparison protocol revision**:

Query duration determines window duration; all boundaries come from duration/grid policy, never saved target times. Map integer sample bounds deterministically. Keep existing candidate asset pools, split assignments, target truth, IoU, K values, tie rules and one-to-one matching.
3. Change the primary selection criterion to **macro candidate query hit@1 across the three source-consistency mixture levels**. Keep the existing secondary criterion, macro controlled performance/pitch hit@5, then lower measured model wall time and lexical method/config identity.
