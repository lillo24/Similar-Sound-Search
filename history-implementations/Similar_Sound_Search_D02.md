## 3. Measure ranking, event coverage and localization separately

Implement an explicit adapter from complete product group results into the D02 prediction schema. Preserve original group rank, every retained representative/support reference, score direction, exclusions, population, shortlist and displayed-card binding. Do not sort a flattened list of windows and call it the product's ranked songs. Lossless packed groups must expand through the validated reader. Context/display envelopes and model padding are not scored audio.

| Measure | Required interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Group hit/recall at 1, 5, 10 and 20 | Use distinct eligible groups and explicit known positives; report query-hit and all-positive-group recovery separately. |
| Known-event recall at the same group cuts | Match actual native scored representatives to truth; retain every target event in the denominator. |
| Localization | Report IoU, truth coverage/containment and start/end error, with matched/missed counts. Conditional boundary error must not hide misses. |
| Shortlist/anchor losses | Separate absent groups, rank-cut losses, representative/cap losses, unreachable grid geometry and scoring losses. |
| Absent targets | Report forced-ranking score/order behavior and validity; no false-accept rate without a real frozen rejection rule. |
| Product/resource behavior | Report job completion, binding/duplicate/exclusion errors, read/encode reuse, latency, memory, metadata and output costs. |

Use one-to-one event matching so one broad passage or many overlapping duplicates cannot count as several recovered events. Preserve the existing half-truth-overlap coverage diagnostic for comparison, and separately report an explicit IoU-based match criterion, such as the existing 0.5 threshold, frozen before inference. Explain the different implications for 2.4s and 4.8s windows. A longer window's containment gain is not automatically more precise localization. Do not pool these metrics under an ambiguous single “accuracy”.

Primary quality reporting must focus on independent-performance/source-consistency cases, separated from exact copies, technical faults and absent targets. Macro-average over declared source families/conditions and retain paired per-case counts. Multiple windows/events from one family are dependent observations; do not manufacture statistical confidence by treating them as independent trials. Descriptive counts and paired improvements/regressions are sufficient for this small suite.

Add independent scalar/native oracles and deliberately wrong, missing, duplicate, reversed-score and out-of-bounds predictions. Verify known answers for group-versus-window ranking, one-to-one matching, exclusions, truncation, ties and missing-case accounting. A technical failure is reported as such and remains a miss/unavailable outcome in the predeclared end-to-end denominator, never dropped to improve quality. Invalid predictions must fail validation rather than become plausible empty success.

Include fixed constant/tie and seeded-random ordering controls over the same eligible groups as diagnostics, without feature inference. Keep their tie policy and seeds explicit. Rankers always return nearest candidates; absent controls do not justify a calibrated presence threshold or confidence percentage. Do not introduce a rejection threshold in this stage merely to obtain a false-positive metric.
