# B02 evidence map

This folder owns prospective B02 policy/integration/protocol and sanitized delivery
evidence. Private signals, feature arrays, worker ledgers and playback bundles live
under `data/runs/separation`, `data/features/separation`, and `reports/local/b02`.
Historical A03–B01 artifacts are inputs and remain immutable.

`policy.json` fixes the finite offline budget before feasibility processing;
`integration.json` declares the narrow shared boundary extension before implementation.
`development.json` and `geometry.json` bind the independently checked support, cues,
candidate truth, settings and promotion gates before separator/matcher inference.
`comparison.json` preserves the completed sixteen-case result and producing identity.

`real-demonstration.json` declares the real source/event/cue before retrieval;
`real-allocation-correction.json` records the refused twelve-second allocation and
the bounded eight-second context without changing the event, cue or limits.
`report-boundary-repair.json` and `report-boundary-checked.json` declare and verify
the later report-only repair against the immutable comparison producer.

`results.json` binds the three real query/result/transform records, unchanged index
receipt and playable bundle. `resources.json` retains final accounting, timings and
disk coverage. `validation.json` records regression, known answers, smoke and separate
native/HTTP/browser checks; `commands.json` retains exact exercised arguments and
failures. `preservation-policy.json` declares the one navigation-document exception,
and `preservation.json` verifies all protected historical files against the baseline.

Policy/integration are prospective declarations; the other records carry canonical
self-digests. Dates and producing code identities remain historical evidence, not
permission to restart an exhausted comparison. [The report](../../reports/B02_SEPARATION.md)
explains original-default findings, limitations, playback startup and B03 handoff.
