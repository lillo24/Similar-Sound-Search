# C01 declarations

`protocol.json` freezes the one development batch, source selection and admission
rule before any C01 inference. Local immutable build plans and snapshots are under
`data/indexes/c01`; mutable progress and the cumulative ledger are under
`data/runs/indexing`. None of these opens an earlier stage's allowance.

`geometry.json` records the actual 72-source identities/native dimensions, real
grid counts and independently inspected saved development insertions/query hashes.
It was committed after native WAV/FFT checks and before any C01 feature inference.

`support-verification.json` retains the raw support tally and independently checks
the strict full-2.4s denominator without reopening predictions; admission is
unchanged. `consumer-integration.json` declares compact references, representation
checks and the final consumer boundary before the fresh delivery audit. Its
unchanged feature-function proofs distinguish producing and consuming versions.
`consumer-publication.json` pins the published consumer hashes and completed
feature counts at that pre-audit boundary; the final audit and delivery results
are recorded in `reports/C01_INDEX_EVIDENCE.json`.
